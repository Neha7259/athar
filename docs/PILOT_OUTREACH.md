# Pilot outreach kit

## Pilot profile

Seek three UAE industrial or logistics facilities with a finance, compliance, HSE, or facilities owner who can provide anonymized Scope 1 and Scope 2 evidence for one reporting year.

## First contact

Ask for a 30-minute workflow interview with the person who currently collects utility bills, fuel invoices, cooling statements, meter logs, and verifier requests.

## Pilot ask

- One facility and one reporting year
- Anonymized evidence only during development
- One reviewer who can confirm extracted fields
- Feedback on the verifier pack and data-quality flags
- Permission to discuss aggregate time saved, without sharing company data

## What Athar will provide

- Evidence hash and retention metadata
- Human-confirmed extraction workflow
- Source Register and traceable calculation preview
- Data-quality flags
- Canonical verifier CSV and HTML pack
- No claim of official IEQT/EAD submission compatibility until official templates are confirmed

## Questions for a verifier partner

1. Which IEQT fields and attachments are required for the current reporting cycle?
2. Which UAE grid factors and GWP basis do you accept?
3. What evidence location reference makes review fastest?
4. Which data-quality flags should block verification?
5. What must appear in the change log and methodology section?
